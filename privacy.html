Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Scroll the Quran ("the app") is built by Dylan Miller. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and we never sell data.

What we collect

The app uses PostHog, an analytics service, to collect anonymous usage events, such as which onboarding screen was viewed, whether a subscription was started, or that an ayah was liked. Events carry a random identifier created on your device, your device model and iOS version, and the app version. They are not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or any other identity, and they are not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Your reading position, liked ayahs, notes, streak, reciter choice and reminder time are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them. Your notes are never uploaded.

Recitation audio

When you play a recitation, the app streams the audio file from EveryAyah.com. Like any web request, this exposes your IP address to that server. No other information is sent.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Notifications

If you allow them, daily reminders and the free-trial reminder are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

You can delete all locally stored data by deleting the app. To ask about analytics data, contact us below.
