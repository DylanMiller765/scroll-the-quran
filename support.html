How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tilawify → Cancel Subscription. You can also tap Manage subscription in the app's Me tab. If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged.

I subscribed but the app is asking me to pay again

Tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the subscription screen, or in Me → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and choose Tilawify.

How do I keep listening with the screen off?

Turn on Keep playing (next ayah) from the reciter menu in the player or in the Me tab. Press play, then lock your phone: recitation carries on ayah after ayah, with controls on the lock screen.

The Qibla compass looks wrong

Move your phone in a figure 8 to calibrate the compass, and keep it away from magnets and metal cases. The Qibla tab needs location access, which you can turn on in Settings → Tilawify → Location.

The recitation won't play

Recitations stream over the internet, so check your connection. If your phone's silent switch is on, recitation still plays; check the volume.

I found a mistake in the text or translation

Please email us the surah and ayah number. Accuracy of the Quran text matters more to us than anything else.
