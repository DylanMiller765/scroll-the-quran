Questions

Is Scroll the Quran free?

The download is free and new subscribers can start with a free trial. After that, a weekly or yearly subscription keeps the app ad-free and tracker-free. Cancel anytime in your iPhone's Settings.

Which translation and reciters are included?

The Arabic Uthmani text (Tanzil) with the Sahih International English translation, and recitations by Mishary Alafasy, Abdur-Rahman As-Sudais, Yasser Al-Dosari, Maher Al-Muaiqly, Mohamed Al-Minshawi and Abdul Basit.

Does it show ads or track me?

No ads and no tracking. We use anonymous, aggregated analytics only to improve the app, and we never sell data. See the privacy policy.

Can I read the whole Quran in order?

Yes. All 6,236 ayahs from Al-Fatiha to An-Nas, your place is always saved, and you can jump to any surah.
